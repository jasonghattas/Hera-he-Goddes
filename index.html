About Hera

A night worthy of a goddess

Hera is a resto bar in Salhiyeh, between Saida and Jezzine. She takes her name from the queen of the Greek gods, and the crown shows in everything we do: the goddess in our emblem, the cocktails that carry her name, and the black-and-gold look of every night.

Dinner starts with fire. Order a fokhara and your steak, chicken or tenderloin arrives raw with fresh vegetables, ready to grill. Then come the cocktails, the shisha and, on the weekend, a DJ at the decks.
